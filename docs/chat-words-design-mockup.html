The ORCH drawer, as it reads

Four lines sent, one per outcome. Under each, the receipt; under the box, who will read the next line. The words are the same everywhere: sent, delivered, seen, answered, listening, not listening.

Every receipt state

Right-aligned under your bubble. Amber is kept for nobody is listening and delivered but not seen in 5 minutes; a busy listener is muted because it is normal.

Under the box

One line, always there. It follows what is typed. A name the board does not know is stopped, never turned into a line to ORCH, and the text stays in the box.
