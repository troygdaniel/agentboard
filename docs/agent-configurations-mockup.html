1 · Pick a configuration (Settings → Agents)

You pick it; the board starts nothing. Applying sets the roster (names, roles, colours) and shows the terminals to start by hand. The files are small and shareable: Import a file… shows what it holds and applies nothing until you confirm.

2 · Rename an agent (the HUD or the agent's row → Rename…)

History, colour, claims and chat come with the name. Counts below are what a rename of one real agent would touch (made-up name). The old name keeps working until its terminal is renamed.

Rename Scout

  • 380 cards it holds or held (5 open stay its), their claims and its colour
  • 77 chat lines it sent or was sent, its read position and who is listening
  • its roster entry, its place in the HUD, its session link for mirrored messages
  • 20 history lines that say "Scout" stay as written: history is history
Nothing changes until you press Rename. agentboard rename Scout Ives --dry-run prints the same counts.
Renamed. Cards and chat now say Ives. In the terminal that was Scout, type /rename Ives and export AGENTBOARD_NAME=Ives. Until then --name Scout still works and shows as Ives. Undo

4 · Day to day: an action waits for your word, every time

Research, drafts and plans are free; a real call, message, payment or form submission is not. The agent writes the exact action as a card in Needs you; your approval is for that one action and nothing else.
