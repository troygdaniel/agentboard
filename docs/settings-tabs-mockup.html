Settings: tabs

Mockup, made-up values

Wide: a vertical tab list, the pane scrolls inside the sheet

The sheet is 720 by 660 (it was 480 wide and as tall as the page; the build makes it min(680px, 100vh - 48px)). General and Texts fit whole; Sound, the old sheet's bulk, lays its layers out in two columns and fits with a short scroll. A tab with nothing in it is not drawn; the Texts tab and its amber dot come from the home plugin.

Narrow (390): a scrolling strip, one column, the sheet is the screen

The strip scrolls sideways with an edge fade, the selected tab scrolls itself into view, and the search box finds a setting on any tab, so no setting depends on seeing its tab. Below 620px the sheet fills the screen.

Search, a deep link and the plugin tabs

Typing in the box replaces the pane with matches (name, one line of what it does, the tab it lives in). Enter or a click opens the tab, scrolls the setting to the top and marks it for a second (a left bar when motion is reduced). /#settings/texts opens the sheet on that tab; the last tab used is remembered per browser.
