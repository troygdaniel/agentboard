# UX review and overhaul, 4 Nov 2026

The ranked list, one line each, with what was done about each (every "Fixed" below landed on the branch overnight; see the morning summary for the commit). Read with the morning summary (`docs/ux-overnight-2026-11-04.md`),
which records every pick and how to reverse it. Line numbers are from main at 543638b, before any of this.

Kept as they are: the look, the sounds, the glide between views, the colour on Doing cards (status: orange running, green review,
red ask; the agent's colour on the stripe and pill).

## Decided before the night (Troy)

1. **Projects go entirely.** Not only the colour: the filter, the pill, the panel select, the bulk assign, the CLI commands, the API, the store, and the docs. The Done summary, which grouped by project, groups by agent instead. Old `tasks.json` files with projects still load; the field is ignored. *Done, in three commits (summary, Python layers, page).*
2. **Every shortcut works on every view** (Board, By agent, Load). *Done, see 7 to 15.*
3. Work goes to the feature branch only; main waits for Troy.
3a. **Card colours** (the card-colors-reconsider card): the status colours stay (they carry meaning) and project colour went with projects. *Done: a Look switch, Calm cards, off by default, drops the running tint so the two can be compared in one click.*

## Bugs, most severe first

4. **Load hides every toast** (`#toast` z-index 20 under `#hud` at 30): "Moved… Undo" and its countdown are invisible, `u` works blind. *Fixed: the toast sits above Load.*
5. **Load hides the error strip** (`#error` is inside `.wrap`, under the overlay), so a failed reply or Mark done from Load is silent; the strip also has no close button. *Fixed: the strip floats above every view, with an × .*
6. **Arrow keys crash** (`TypeError` at `navigate`, 2982) when the focused card is not in the grid: a Load Todo card, a Later card, a Chat chip, or a board card under Load. *Fixed: a card outside the grid is treated as no card.*
7. **On Load, each poll moves keyboard focus back to the hidden board copy** of the card (`render` 1616 only looks in `#board`), so arrow navigation resets every second. *Fixed: focus is put back in the pane you were in.*
8. **No way to move a card to Todo from Load**: Alt+arrows are blocked twice, drag is off, the bulk bar is under the overlay. *Fixed: Alt+← / → move the focused card between Load's Todo and Doing, as on the Board.*
9. **Arrows and j/k on Load walk only Doing**; Todo cards are unreachable, and if Doing is empty the keys do nothing. *Fixed: the grid is Todo then Doing, ← and → cross between them.*
10. **Alt+arrows fire while typing** in a card's reply box (no typing check at 3365): Option+← moves the card instead of the cursor. *Fixed: typing wins.*
11. **Esc in the panel's reply box, subtask or blocker field closes the whole panel** (2139 has no typing check). *Fixed: Esc leaves the field first; the next Esc closes the panel.*
12. **Esc with the shortcuts sheet or Settings open over a panel closes the panel, not the sheet.** *Fixed: the top layer closes first.*
13. **`m` and `s` do nothing inside Settings** (opening it focuses a checkbox, which counts as typing). *Fixed: a checkbox, radio or range is not typing.*
14. **Alt+arrows do nothing in By agent** and the sheet says "Board only". *Fixed: ↑ / ↓ reorder within a lane and ← / → move between Todo and the lane, as on the Board; the sheet says so.*
15. **A selection made in Load is invisible** (`renderSelection` paints only `#board`, the bulk bar is under the overlay) and Esc closes Load instead of clearing it. *Fixed: the selection paints wherever the card is shown and Esc clears it first; the bulk bar sits above Load.*
16. **A keyboard user on a button inside a card drops to `<body>` on every changed poll** (focus is only restored to the card itself). *Fixed: focus is restored to the same control by its role and card.*
17. **The panel's native select, the blocker datalist, an armed "Click again to delete" and a "copied" label reset every minute** (`renderPanel` rebuilds `#p-body` on the minute tick). *Fixed: no rebuild while focus is inside the panel body.*
18. **The once-a-minute redraw wipes any text you have selected** in the panel, so a long ask cannot be copied reliably. *Fixed: the rebuild waits while a selection exists (in the panel, and on the board, though a draggable card's text cannot be selected in Chromium anyway).*
19. **Typing in a reply box freezes the status pill, the asks and the chat** (`load()` returns before `setStatus`, `checkAsks`, `loadChat`). *Fixed: only the board redraw waits for typing.*
20. **The header reflows** when the summary counts appear or change, when ORCH or the board switch show up late, or when the status pill changes text, and through the ResizeObserver that moves Load's top edge. *Fixed: the header does not wrap at desktop widths, the summary shrinks before it wraps, the late buttons reserve their slot, numbers are tabular.*
21. **Opening the bulk bar shrinks every column by about 74px.** *Fixed: the bulk bar floats over the board's foot.*
22. **Every card glides for 420ms when any card above it changes height** (the FLIP runs on every changed render). *Fixed: a card glides only when its column or order changed.*
23. **Load's columns scroll back to the top on every open.** *Fixed: each pane keeps its scroll across opens.*
24. **The Later drawer says "Nothing parked" when a filter is on** (it uses the filtered list). *Fixed: the drawer and its count ignore the filter.*
25. **Ellipsised text with no tooltip**: the card's time/stale text, subtask rows, toast titles, chat card chips, the Load pill's name. *Fixed: a title on each.*
26. **A Doing card with a report hides the Report chip** (shown only for ask, review, done) and the chip moves between the top row and the foot. *Fixed: shown whenever a report exists, in the top row.*
27. **"Show N more" in Done has no way back.** *Fixed: "Show fewer".*
28. **Sound on or off is invisible** since the SND/AMB buttons left the header; only Settings or `s` tells you. *Fixed: the Settings gear shows a muted badge while sound is off.*
29. **Settings says "plays with AMB"** (no AMB control exists) and files Motion, a visual preference, under "Sound for every view". *Fixed: a Look section holds Motion and the new look switches; the note and heading read right.*
30. **Hovering an agent pill flickers on every changed poll** (the highlight is rebuilt with the board). *Fixed: the hovered agent is re-applied after a redraw.*
31. **The Later drawer's input is rebuilt on every changed render** (caret and IME composition lost). *Fixed: the form is built once; only the list is replaced.*
32. **Load's telemetry rows lift on hover like cards but are not clickable.** *Fixed: no lift unless the row opens something.*
33. **`?`, `n`, `l`, `a`, `e` and the arrows are dropped in the pop-out window** and the sheet does not say so. *Fixed where they make sense (`?`, the arrows, `e`); the sheet says the pop-out has no board for `n`, `l`, `a`.*
34. **`c` ignores an open sheet while `d` checks for one**; a telemetry row under the pointer counts as a card for `d`. *Fixed: both check, and only cards aim.*
35. **A chat card chip opens the panel over the open Chat drawer** (two Esc presses to clear). *Fixed: opening a panel closes Chat like it closes Later.*
36. **The Done ribbon's week count moves the day bars when it gains a digit; the Later button grows at 100.** *Fixed: reserved widths.*
37. **The shortcuts sheet is out of date**: Space, Cmd/Ctrl+Enter, Enter to add a subtask or blocker, Esc in the editor are missing; "Board only" for Alt+arrows is wrong after 14. *Fixed: the sheet matches the handlers, and a test keeps it so.*

## Left alone, and why

38. **Chat rebuilds its body on every new message** (focus on Approve/Decline lost, an expanded thread jumps). An append-only renderer is a day's work with its own risks; noted for a card of its own.
39. **By agent lanes re-divide the width when a lane appears.** Animating grid tracks needs a different layout; the Motion layer already slides the new lane in.
40. **The lane's "quiet" rule differs from Load's server state.** Unifying means choosing one clock; left for Troy to pick which should win.
41. **Card descriptions and details stay hidden on cards** (only the panel shows them). That was a deliberate "less noise" change on 28 Oct; a "has details" glyph would add exactly the visual info Troy is cutting.
41a. **The hidden Unassigned Doing cards on By agent** were not in the audit but turned up while testing: fixed (an Unassigned lane, last).
42. **The panel's description sits low** (after the timeline). Moving it is taste; left as is.
