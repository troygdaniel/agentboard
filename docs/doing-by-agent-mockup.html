Recommendation: A, Doing expands in place

It answers the question in one tap without leaving the board. The thing you want to see is "who is on what, and who needs me", and that is Doing split by agent; Todo and Done still sit at the edges as rails, so the queue and the week never vanish and dragging still works. Five agents get real columns at desktop width; the idle agents are one strip, never a column, so the view is always the agents that matter right now. On a phone it degrades to a grouped list with sticky agent headers, which is what a phone does best.

B is the wall-display answer, and the HUD already is that. A page of its own is right for a second screen, but seven columns at 1440px are cramped, Todo and Done leave the page, and the HUD's per-agent rows already show who is on what with a chart each. If you want B, the honest version is "the HUD with cards instead of charts".

C is the weakest. A drawer makes the five agents a list, so ask and review stop being a glance across the top and become a read down the state words; it also covers Doing on a laptop, the one column it is about.
