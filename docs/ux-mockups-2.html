1Quick replies on asks

static/index.html
Most asks are a choice. The board reads the question and offers its options as chips (3 or 5? becomes 3 and 5; yes/no, a, b or c, numbered lists likewise), plus three that fit any ask: Go ahead, Hold, Your call. One tap sends the reply the way the box does today (/api/reply, the agent reads it with agentboard answer). The typed box stays for everything else. Same chips on the Your move row, the card and the panel.

2Hand to… on quiet cards

static/index.html · store.py (one method, same hook as Assign)
A card whose agent went quiet needs a new pair of hands, not a trip through Todo. Hand to… on the Your move row and in the panel opens the same picker as Assign, idle agents first. One pick: the card goes back to Todo under the new agent, the old agent is released, the card log says so, and the chat line tells ORCH: ORCH: Troy handed <id> from AGENT-4 to AGENT-5; hand it over. Also on any Doing card in the panel, for the times you just want it elsewhere.

3Project rail

static/index.html
The project select becomes a thin rail on the left: each project with its colour, a stacked bar of its open and finished work (todo · doing · done this week), the count, and a red dot when one of its cards needs you. Click a project to filter the whole board (strip included), click again for all. Collapses to the dots on narrow screens; the select stays as the keyboard path.

4Edit in place on the card

static/index.html · server.py (one route: /api/edit)
Today a card's title and description can only change from the terminal. In the panel, the title and the description become editable where they are (click, or e); Save or ⌘↵, Esc cancels, and the log gets one line. The subtask list ends in an add row, and a Blocked by row takes a card by name with the same picker the filter uses. Agents are never edited this way; only the card's words and shape.

5Desktop notifications from the tab

static/index.html
A bell in the top bar. On, the board uses the browser's own Notification API (local, no service, no new dependency) to ping you when a card needs an answer, is ready for sign-off, or its agent went quiet, but only while the tab is in the background; in front, the strip and the sounds already do it. Click a notification and the card's panel opens. Replaces the [notify] osascript route for the common case, and works in any browser.
